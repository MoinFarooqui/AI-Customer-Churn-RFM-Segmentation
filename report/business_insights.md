📊 Business Insights
1. Customer Base and Revenue

The analysis covers approximately 49,673 customers and generates around 205 million in total revenue, indicating a substantial customer transaction dataset suitable for customer behavior and churn analysis.

Business Insight: The large customer base provides enough transaction history to identify meaningful purchasing patterns and customer segments.

2. Customer Churn

Approximately 20% of customers have churned, while nearly 80% remain active.

Business Insight: Although the majority of customers are retained, the churned customer segment represents a significant potential loss of future revenue. Identifying at-risk customers before they leave should therefore be a key business priority.

3. High-Risk Customers

Our Random Forest churn prediction model identified 30 high-risk customers.

Business Insight: These customers should be prioritized for immediate retention campaigns, as targeted intervention may help prevent potential customer loss.

4. Product Category Revenue

The product categories generate different levels of revenue, with categories such as Books and Clothing appearing among the strongest revenue contributors in the dashboard.

Business Insight: High-performing categories should be further analyzed to understand which customer segments drive their revenue and whether similar strategies can be applied to lower-performing categories.

5. Revenue Trends

The revenue trend shows fluctuations across the analyzed time period rather than a perfectly consistent growth pattern.

Business Insight: Changes in customer purchasing behavior over time can help businesses identify periods of stronger and weaker performance and plan marketing campaigns accordingly.

🧩 Customer Segmentation Insights
6. Customer Behavior is Not Uniform

Using RFM Analysis and K-Means Clustering, customers were grouped based on:

Recency of purchases
Purchase frequency
Total monetary value

Business Insight: Customers should not be treated as one homogeneous group. Different segments require different engagement and retention strategies.

7. High-Value Customers

Customers with relatively high purchase frequency and monetary value represent important business segments.

Business Recommendation: These customers should receive loyalty benefits, exclusive offers, and personalized experiences to maintain their engagement.

8. Low-Engagement Customers

Customers with high recency values—meaning they have not purchased recently—and lower purchasing activity may require re-engagement.

Business Recommendation: Personalized discounts, reminder campaigns, and product recommendations could help reactivate these customers.

🤖 Churn Prediction Insights
9. Predictive Analytics Enables Proactive Retention

Instead of waiting for customers to churn, the Random Forest model estimates the probability of churn for individual customers.

Business Insight: This allows the business to move from a reactive approach to a proactive retention strategy.

🧠 GenAI Retention Insights
10. AI Can Support Personalized Customer Retention

The GenAI retention engine generates recommendations for selected high-risk customers using information such as:

Purchase behavior
Customer spending
Churn probability
Customer segment

Business Insight: Combining Machine Learning with Generative AI helps transform predictions into actionable business recommendations.
